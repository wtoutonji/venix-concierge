## 2. What the site can honestly support

Verified from the project's real copy (see §5 coverage table) and handoff records:

- **Offered:** chauffeur-driven private transfers (hourly / full-day / multi-day), airport
  transfers, business and private event transport, delegation/diplomatic transfers,
  concierge services, weddings, a four-vehicle Mercedes-Benz fleet (S-Class, E-Class,
  V-Class, Sprinter). Protection, private flights and embassy services are described as
  **coordinated through authorized partners**, subject to assessment and licensing.
- **Not supported anywhere in the project (do not target or claim):** BMW 7 Series
  (not in the fleet), armoured vehicles, limousines, transfers across Europe or a defined
  intercity network, "VIP line" as a product. Claiming them for SEO would create
  unsupported factual claims.
- **Bracketed placeholders / pending approvals** (operating area, response time, legal
  wording) remain pending; see §12.

## 7. Cannibalization analysis

**English.** Home, Services and Contact all revolve around *chauffeur*. Home vs Services is
the live risk (Home mentions airport, events and delegations too, with ~900 words).
Separation: Home = company/umbrella ("chauffeur and concierge service"), Services =
hire/transfer offers, Contact = branded/navigational. Fleet's *luxury* phrase competes
with nothing today because Home never uses it — but it also isn't supported by Fleet copy.

**Polish.** Same stem overlap with *szofer* across Home (focus), Services (focus,
"transfery z szoferem"), Contact (focus, "zamów szofera") and titles on Home/About/Fleet/
Contact all containing "szofer". That is the strongest cannibalization risk of any
language: five titles share one stem. Proposed separation in §6. Client term "Usługi
Concierge" must not be assigned to both Home and a future concierge page.

**Russian.** Home (*персональный водитель*) vs Services (*VIP трансфер* / proposed
*аренда автомобиля с водителем*) overlap via *водитель*; Contact (*заказать трансфер*)
vs Services (*трансфер*) overlap via *трансфер*. Proposed separation in §6.

**Future-page risk (all languages).** Anchors (`/services/#airport`, `#events`,
`#weddings`, `#concierge`, `#delegations`, `#chauffeur`) are sections of Services; they are
not independent indexable pages. If dedicated pages are created later, Services must stop
using those phrases as its own focus and should link to the new pages ("hub" role), or
the pages will compete.
